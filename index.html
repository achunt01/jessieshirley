A visit

Visits are about three hours in your home. On massage days, 60 to 90 minutes is massage on my table, and the rest of the time goes to the things below.

Fig. 1–6

Baby care is light: holding the baby while you sleep, or a diaper change. I don't do overnight newborn care or sleep training.

Most postpartum doulas focus on the baby. I focus on the mother: your rest, your recovery and how you're really doing.

Your body has just been through a lot, and it heals better when someone is looking after you. Because I'm also a massage therapist, that care includes hands-on work, not just help around the house.

Questions

How is this different from a regular postpartum doula?

The focus is on you rather than the newborn. And because I'm a certified massage therapist, postpartum massage is included in Restore and Deep Recovery.

Will you take care of my baby?

Lightly. I can hold the baby while you nap or change a diaper. No overnight care or sleep training.

When should I book?

During pregnancy if you can, since I only take a few clients around each due date.

What if my baby comes early or late?

We'll adjust. That's why packages can start any time in the first 6 to 8 weeks.

Can I get a massage after a C-section?

Check with your doctor or midwife first. Once you're cleared, I'll work gently around anything that's still healing.

What if someone is sick?

Let me know at least 24 hours ahead if you can and we'll reschedule. I'll do the same.

Pets or older kids?

No problem at all.

What don't you do?

I'm not a medical provider, lactation consultant or therapist, and I don't give medical advice. I'll refer you to people I trust.

What's your cancellation policy?

Package visits: reschedule 24+ hours ahead and we'll move the visit within your six weeks. With less notice, the visit counts as used, unless you or the baby are sick.

Single massages: free to cancel 24+ hours ahead, 50% inside 24 hours, full price within an hour of the appointment.
